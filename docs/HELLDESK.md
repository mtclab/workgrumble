# Workgrumble: Helldesk

A first/third-person dungeon crawler built on the office sim's world. Doom's
pace and face-in-the-status-bar, Elder Scrolls' gear, perks and encumbrance,
and every ticket in it is a real ticket from `src/world/tickets`.

Play it: `npm run dev`, then open `/crawler.html`. It ships in the same
`vite build` as the office sim (a second page), so it sits behind the same
Worker door as everything else.

## The pitch

You are Pat Pending, IT Support Analyst. The building has turned hostile: every
floor of Workgrumble Ltd is a dungeon of cubicles, meeting rooms, kitchens and
server rooms, and the people in it all have a problem. Fight your way up five
floors, resolve the floor's boss in the corner office, and take the lift. Beat
the CEO and you finally buy the farm (the office sim's win goal). Then the goats'
Wi-Fi goes down and Overtime begins (endless, scaling floors).

## People

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Walk-up users** | Melee "quick questions". Sometimes log a ticket on you. |
| **Callers** (headsets) | Keep their distance and throw paper tickets. A hit adds their ticket to your queue. |
| **Gold-tier customers** | Tanky, throw GOLD tickets (shorter SLA, pay double). |
| **Reply-All storms** | Small fast envelopes that swarm and pop. |
| **Paper jams** | Guard printers, spray paper. |
| **Managers** | The encumbrance class. A slowing aura ("Got a sec?"), meeting invites that root you in place, action items that add 6 kg each to your pack, and they delegate users onto you. An SLA breach *escalates* - a manager comes to find you. |
| **Office ladies** (Brenda from Accounts, Linda from Reception...) | Healers. Passive tea-and-sympathy heals when you're near, a gift of cake or biscuits, and they'll take your action items off you ("I'll minute them, love"). They also receive laptop deliveries. |
| **Helpers** | Recruit with E. Sysadmins fire RTFM beams, Gary from Security stuns, the Intern fetches coffee and ammo. |
| **Bosses** | Derek (Team Leader), Karen (VP Customer Success), Gordon (Head of Procurement), The Auditor, and Sir Reginald Workgrumble (CEO). Each has its own pattern set: meeting-invite fans, gold-ticket spirals, escalation shockwaves, purchase-order bombs, budget freezes, compliance lasers, All-Hands summons, golden-parachute teleports. Enraged at half health. |

Beating someone in combat *resolves* them: they cheer, float off, drop loot
and Rep, and their ticket leaves your queue.

## Computers (the office sim's OS, kept)

Every terminal boots WorkgrumbleOS - the same beige-box, teal-accent desktop
as the office sim. The game pauses while you're logged on.

- **Tickets** - your queue, with SLA timers. Read the real ticket, then pick
  the fix: the right answer is one of that ticket's real fix paths, the decoys
  are other tickets' fixes. Wrong costs sanity and SLA. You can also pick up
  more tickets from the backlog for Rep.
- **KB Wiki** - the real knowledge-base articles; the ones for your queued
  tickets are pinned.
- **Mail (Tasks)** - pick up new tasks (close N tickets, resolve N people, fix
  the printer, deliver a laptop) and claim finished ones.
- **Internal IT** - requisition gear for Rep. Also at the physical Internal IT
  counter on every floor (Morag will see you now).
- **Career & Kit** - equipment slots, supplies, perks.
- **cat_pictures.url** - slack off for sanity. Sometimes a manager catches you.

## Gear, perks, encumbrance

Tools (weapons): Swingline Stapler, Model M Keyboard, Label Maker, Cat6 Whip,
Compressed Air Duster, Rubber Duck Launcher, Toner Cannon, The Power Cycle,
sudo Hammer. Armour slots (head, body, feet, trinket) include the Kevlar
Cardigan, Noise-Cancelling Headphones (halve manager auras), Out-of-Office
Robe (immune to meeting invites) and the Rubber Duck (shows a ticket's root
cause). Supplies: coffee, Grumble Energy (wired, then the crash), biscuits,
cake, sticky notes (clear an action item) and, not during probation, beer.

Everything has a weight. Over your carry limit you're slow and can't sprint -
and managers are how you get there. Each level-up is a promotion up the title
ladder and a perk point (Infinite Patience, Touch Typing, Strong Back, Soft
Skills, Command Line Fu, Caffeine Tolerance, Invisible at Desk, Teflon,
Delegation).

## Controls

WASD move · Mouse look · LMB use tool · RMB shove · Shift sprint · Space jump ·
E interact · Q quick-use supplies · 1-9 / wheel tools · V first/third person ·
Tab backpack · M automap · Esc pause.

## Code

All of it lives in `src/crawler/` and depends on nothing in the office sim at
runtime:

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `content/tickets.ts` | GENERATED by `node scripts/crawler-content.mjs` from `src/world/tickets` + `src/world/kb`. Re-run after changing ticket content. |
| `level.ts` | Seeded floor generation (rooms, corridors, props), grid collision, line of sight, flow-field navigation. |
| `entities.ts` | Every NPC: stats, AI, boss patterns. |
| `game.ts` | The loop, combat, projectiles, tickets, quests, floors, screens. |
| `os.ts` | WorkgrumbleOS terminal. |
| `hud.ts` | Status bar, Pat's face, minimap, automap. |
| `player.ts`, `characters.ts` | Camera rigs and the box-people. |
| `items.ts`, `state.ts` | Gear/perk tables and the save (localStorage). |
| `audio.ts`, `textures.ts` | Synthesised sound and canvas-painted textures - no asset files. |

`crawler.test.ts` proves 150 generated floors are fully connected with every
interactable reachable, plus the save/perk/encumbrance maths.
