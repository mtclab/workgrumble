# Exit endings research: every ending is getting out of IT

Dated 2026-08-20. Research for the owner's ending reframe: the win condition
set = ways OUT of IT work. Confirmed set going in: the farm (canonical),
moving abroad (Thailand-class), starting your own business, FIRE. This doc
collects the real-world exit archetypes with evidence they are patterns (not
one blog post), the price/threshold SHAPE each one wants as a game knob, how
other games make escape endings felt, and a recommended shortlist.

House rule applied throughout: no fabricated figures. Every concrete number
below is a "commonly cited as" shape with a source; anything that would
appear in shipped copy is marked **DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED** and must be
re-checked (or, better, replaced by game-currency numbers) before it reaches
a player-facing string. The game should price endings in its OWN salary
units anyway - the real figures are here to get the RATIOS right.

Anchors in existing design: `docs/design/e11-endings.md` (escape fund fires
at arc boundaries, endings chosen by state not menu, price tags per ending =
the difficulty select, farm hardest), `docs/DESIGN_POC.md` ("farm price on
the wall", daily salary, payslip as joke surface).

## 1. Recurring exit archetypes

The pattern behind all of them: tech burnout is mass-scale (surveys put it
around half or more of tech workers - one year-over-year survey jump from
~45% to ~56%, and "73-83% of developers at some point" claims circulate;
exact numbers move, the SHAPE is "most of the industry fantasizes about the
door"). The fantasies people actually act on cluster tightly. Sources:
[Dice tech sentiment](https://www.dice.com/hiring/recruitment/reports/tech-sentiment-report),
[Lenny's 2026 tech survey](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-tech-workers-are-feeling-in-2026),
[ACM on 57% burnout](https://cacmb4.acm.org/careers/229074-57-of-tech-workers-are-suffering-from-job-burnout).

### 1.1 The farm / homestead (CANONICAL - already the game's north star)

- **What it is**: quit, buy land, grow food, keep animals. The single most
  documented tech-exit fantasy in existence.
- **Evidence it is a pattern**: it is literally a genre. "Cottagecore
  programmers" essays analyze why the fantasy is evergreen regardless of the
  tech job market ([tjmorley](https://tjmorley.com/blogposts/cottagecoreprogrammers.html)),
  programmer-humor has a standing ["goat farming" meme tag](https://programmerhumor.io/memes/goat-farming),
  a 22-year Microsoft veteran going goose farmer went viral as "living the
  dream" ([cheezburger writeup](https://cheezburger.com/25761029/hes-living-the-dream-corporate-tech-bro-gets-kudos-for-quitting-22-year-career-becoming-goose-farmer)),
  and the whole "Corporate American Homesteader" vlog economy exists to
  narrate leaving Corporate America for land. Nuance from the same sources:
  many real ones keep "one foot in the software world" - the pure exit is
  rare, which is exactly why it deserves to be the game's hardest ending.
- **Price/threshold shape**: the biggest lump sum in the game, full stop.
  Real-world shape: land + house + equipment + working capital until the
  farm feeds you. US hobby-farm listings average in the high six figures
  (one aggregator cites ~$879k average listing) and "buy land + start a
  5-10 acre smallholding" guides land in the $150k-$300k+ range
  ([LandSearch](https://www.landsearch.com/hobby-farm/united-states),
  [dojobusiness](https://dojobusiness.com/blogs/news/how-much-cost-start-a-farm)) -
  **DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED** (regional, moving, and the game should use its
  own currency). Game knob: farm price = N years of CURRENT salary, and the
  price drifts UP as the player's salary rises (nicer district, the brochure
  gets glossier) - lifestyle inflation as the difficulty curve, exactly the
  July sketch. The farm is the only ending that costs "everything you could
  ever save".
- **Tone note**: play it 100% straight. This is the grandfather's-letter
  dream; the joke is that the ticket queue exists, never that the goats do.
  Epilogue comedy comes from the job's absence: the rooster files no
  tickets, escalates nothing, and is still more reasonable than the L2 queue.

### 1.2 Moving abroad, Thailand-class (CONFIRMED)

- **What it is**: lifestyle emigration to a low-cost country; the beach
  co-working cliché, the early-retiree in Chiang Mai.
- **Evidence it is a pattern**: an entire visa CLASS now exists for it -
  Thailand's DTV (Destination Thailand Visa) was created for exactly this
  person, and the retire-in-Thailand content industry is enormous
  ([roafly DTV guide](https://www.roafly.com/blog/thailand-digital-nomad-visa),
  [Asia Lifestyle Magazine](https://www.asialifestylemagazine.com/retiring-in-thailand-costs-work-options/)).
- **Price/threshold shape**: the interesting one - it is NOT a big lump.
  Real shape: a modest proof-of-savings bar (DTV commonly cited: 500k THB,
  ~$14.5k, in the bank) plus an ONGOING income that clears the local cost
  of living (commonly cited: $1,500-$2,500/mo comfortable single-person
  budget; retirement visa proper is 50+ only, LTR wants ~$80k/yr passive) -
  all **DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED** (visa rules churn yearly; the game should
  caricature "a savings bar + a monthly income stream", never quote baht).
  Game knob: the CHEAPEST capital threshold in the set, but gated on an
  income SOURCE, not a pile - e.g. requires an unlocked side-gig /
  freelance contact / passive stream that survives the resignation. Low
  fund bar, extra prerequisite. That makes it mid-difficulty and
  mechanically distinct from FIRE.
- **Tone note**: the comedy target is the job's timezone dying, not the
  destination or its people. No "cheap paradise" smirk, no lazy expat
  jokes; the epilogue beat is the laptop staying shut through a standup
  happening 8 timezones away. Keep the country generic-or-named with
  respect ("somewhere the ping to the office is mercifully unplayable").

### 1.3 Starting your own business (CONFIRMED) - recommend: the café flavor

- **What it is**: quit to run your own thing. Real-world exits split into
  two textures that the game should NOT conflate:
  - **The physical dream**: café / bakery / bookstore / board-game café /
    brewery / workshop. Documented as THE white-collar daydream, complete
    with an advice-column genre of "should I quit and open a bookstore?"
    reality checks ([Ask a Manager](https://www.askamanager.org/2025/04/should-i-quit-my-job-and-open-a-bookstore-coworker-was-disciplined-for-sharing-info-everyone-already-knew-and-more.html),
    [board-game café guide](https://atouchofbusiness.com/startup-ideas/board-game-cafe/)).
    Blind/HN exit threads name chef, brewer, puzzle company, organic farm,
    landscaping ("anything that creates order feels amazing") -
    ([Blind thread](https://www.teamblind.com/post/what-do-you-think-youd-do-if-you-leave-the-tech-industry-mdoufaxu)).
  - **The indie-SaaS flavor**: "ramen profitable" - revenue covers basic
    burn, so you can quit without starving; the community milestone is ~$5k
    MRR, and the sobering stat circulating is that the median profitable
    micro-SaaS (~$4.2k MRR) pays well under half a dev salary
    ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/what-is-your-ramen-profitable-goal-8b88855378),
    [indie masterminds on $5k MRR](https://www.indiemasterminds.com/articles/what-is-the-5k-mrr-milestone)) -
    **DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED**. NOTE: this flavor arguably fails the
    owner's frame - you are still at a computer taking tickets, they are
    just called "support emails" now. See open question Q-A.
- **Price/threshold shape** (café flavor): mid-size lump - real shape runs
  from ~$15k-$25k (home-based) through $30k-$75k (small storefront) to
  $100k-$200k+ (full café) ([Homebase](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-a-bakery),
  [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-bakery)) -
  **DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED**. Game knob: roughly a THIRD of the farm price,
  plus a non-money prerequisite (a "plan" the player assembled - e.g. a
  side-project meter filled during lunch-slack windows, so slacking with
  intent becomes investment). Mid-high difficulty.
- **Tone note**: never joke that the café will fail (the reality-check
  genre is full of "it loses money" - the game does not say that to the
  player's dream). The joke is what the player brings FROM the job: the
  espresso machine gets a hostname; the pastry case is the first queue in
  years where everything gets closed by 3pm.

### 1.4 FIRE - retire on savings (CONFIRMED)

- **What it is**: financial independence, retire early; save a multiple of
  your annual spending, live off withdrawals, answer to no one.
- **Evidence it is a pattern**: a named movement with its own vocabulary,
  heavily overrepresented in tech (Blind is wall-to-wall "can I FatFIRE"
  threads). Conventions: the "rule of 25" (25x annual EXPENSES, from the
  4%-withdrawal Trinity-study tradition); lean FIRE commonly framed as
  ~$40k/yr spend => ~$1M; fat FIRE ~$100k/yr => ~$2.5M+; more conservative
  camps say 30-33x for very early retirement
  ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/investing/financial-independence-retire-early),
  [SmartAsset](https://smartasset.com/retirement/fire-number),
  [Motley Fool lean-vs-fat](https://www.fool.com/retirement/2025/06/05/lean-fire-vs-fat-fire-which-path-fits-your-values)) -
  the multiplier is safe to caricature, the dollar figures are
  **DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED**.
- **Price/threshold shape**: the elegant one - a MULTIPLE OF THE PLAYER'S
  OWN SPENDING, not a fixed price. Game knob: FIRE number = 25x the
  player's rolling annual burn. Every beer, browser game, and
  stress-relief purchase raises the bar; frugal play lowers it. This is
  the golden-handcuffs mechanism as a mechanic - the trap is documented as
  lifestyle expanding to match income until leaving feels impossible
  ([Kompiq on golden handcuffs](https://kompiq.com/blog/golden-handcuffs-career-trap),
  [Annie Wright](https://anniewright.com/golden-handcuffs-when-your-salary-is-a-cage/)).
  Difficulty self-tunes: a high-earning high-spending player chases a
  receding number (fat FIRE), a monk run hits lean FIRE early. Should sit
  between café and farm for a typical run.
- **Tone note**: funny-but-true is built in - the payslip already being a
  joke surface, the FIRE tracker can live next to it. The joke is the
  25x arithmetic applied to the player's actual sins ("at your current
  beer velocity, freedom arrives in 214 years"). Never mock retiring
  itself; mock what the job made the player spend to survive it.

### 1.5 The trade - electrician/plumber (NEW - strongest addition)

- **What it is**: retrain into a skilled trade. Real problems, physical
  results, the workday ends when the pipe stops leaking.
- **Evidence it is a pattern**: the canonical HN thread is literally titled
  "Anyone else feel the constant urge to leave the field and become a
  plumber?" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=30323561)); the
  earn-while-you-learn apprenticeship pipeline is well documented (4-5
  years, starting around half a journeyman's wage, rising annually)
  ([UTI](https://www.uti.edu/blog/education/electrician-apprenticeship-vs-trade-school),
  [Washington Informer](https://www.washingtoninformer.com/trade-career-learning-earn-models/));
  even Jensen Huang telling the world it needs hundreds of thousands of
  electricians feeds the fantasy
  ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-says-145838012.html)).
- **Price/threshold shape**: the NOVEL knob - not a capital pile but an
  ENDURANCE test. Real shape: near-zero tuition (apprenticeships pay YOU),
  but years at roughly half pay - commonly cited ~50% of journeyman wage,
  ~$15-$26/hr starts (**DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED**). Game knob: requires a
  modest buffer (cover the pay cut) plus ACCEPTING a low-income period -
  e.g. the offer appears once savings cover N weeks of halved income, and
  taking it is priced in time-and-humility, not cash. This gives the
  ending set a second AXIS (money vs endurance) instead of one ladder of
  lump sums. Low-mid capital difficulty, but emotionally "expensive".
- **Tone note**: the register writes itself and punches only at IT: "the
  water heater has no roadmap. It will not pivot. When it is fixed, it
  STAYS fixed." Do not joke that trades are simple or lesser - the whole
  point is the respect the fantasy carries.

### 1.6 Teaching / the service exit (NEW)

- **What it is**: retrain to teach (math, IT, shop class) - the "do
  something that matters, see the faces" exit. Nursing is the sibling
  archetype (accelerated BSN programs, ~16 months, explicitly marketed at
  career changers -
  [Rockhurst](https://onlinedegrees.rockhurst.edu/blog/nursing-as-a-second-career)).
- **Evidence it is a pattern**: exit threads recur on "teach math or
  programming" ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/what-do-you-think-youd-do-if-you-leave-the-tech-industry-mdoufaxu));
  first-person "why I switched from software engineering to teaching"
  essays exist as a genre ([csclassroom substack](https://csclassroom.substack.com/p/why-i-switched-from-software-engineering),
  [builtin](https://builtin.com/software-engineering-perspectives/leaving-software-development-to-teach-coding)).
  Notably the pipeline runs BOTH ways (teachers flee into tech for the
  money) - which is honest texture: this exit takes a permanent pay cut on
  purpose.
- **Price/threshold shape**: small lump (credential cost + a transition
  buffer) gated on LOW PERSONAL BURN - the ending is only offered if the
  player's lifestyle fits a teacher's salary. Mechanically the mirror of
  FIRE: FIRE rewards frugality with an earlier number, teaching REQUIRES
  frugality as the entry ticket. Cheap in money, priced in lifestyle.
- **Tone note**: warmest ending in the set; play it fully straight. The
  only joke allowed is at the old job's expense: "you now explain things
  for a living to people who actually want to know."

### 1.7 The van / the long walkabout (NEW - the ending that isn't)

- **What it is**: buy the van, drive; or the one-way ticket and a rucksack.
  The sabbatical stretched until it becomes a lifestyle.
- **Evidence it is a pattern**: sabbatical-finance guides for tech workers
  are their own genre (commonly cited: $30k-$50k for a few months plus
  job-search runway - **DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED**;
  [Woven Capital](https://www.wovencapital.net/how-to-financially-plan-for-a-sabbatical-a-guide-for-tech-professionals-ready-for-a-career-break/)),
  and Blind's "saved $250k at 24, can I quit and travel" threads recur
  monthly ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/saved-250k-at-24-can-i-quit-tech-and-go-travel-world-m5h745ts)).
  The adjacent outdoor flavor - park ranger, ski season, dive instructor -
  is documented as low-pay, low-barrier seasonal work people take ON
  PURPOSE after desks ([Bearfoot Theory](https://bearfoottheory.com/outdoor-career-paths/),
  [NPS career tips](https://www.nps.gov/ozar/learn/management/career-tips.htm)).
- **Price/threshold shape**: the CHEAPEST bar in the game - a vehicle plus
  months of runway. But it is honest to make it finite: real sabbaticals
  end. Design opportunity: this is the soft ending / new-game+ seam - the
  epilogue rolls, the odometer climbs, and the save can offer "the money
  ran out in October" as the canonical NG+ re-entry (back to the desk,
  records kept, badge earned). e11's Q2 (trophy vs NG+) has a natural home
  here. Alternatively the seasonal-outdoors flavor makes it a TRUE ending
  at a higher frugality gate (ranger pay forever = teaching-style low-burn
  requirement).
- **Tone note**: never frame the return as failure - frame it as the
  player having proof the door opens. The joke: the out-of-office
  autoreply still running, years later, unanswered.

### Archetypes checked and NOT recommended as separate endings

- **Woodworking / craft business**: real but the sources are unusually
  honest that nearly everyone supplements the income
  ([Wood Whisperer](https://thewoodwhisperer.com/articles/why-i-dont-offer-woodworking-business-advice/));
  fold it in as a FLAVOR of the own-business ending (the workshop skin),
  not a fifth economy.
- **Cop/detective, landscaping, brewing, puzzle company**: each appears in
  exit threads, none recurs strongly enough alone; they are one-off color
  for epilogue variety or NPC backstory ("the last person at this desk
  left to become a detective").
- **Nursing**: real and strong, but overlaps teaching's knob shape
  (credential + pay-cut acceptance); recommend ONE service-exit ending
  with selectable flavor rather than two endings with identical economics.
- **MSP / IT consultancy**: the most COMMON real "own business" for IT
  people - and precisely NOT an escape from IT. Great material for the
  game's employer roster (the player can WORK at one), wrong as an ending
  under the owner's frame.

## 2. How other games make escape endings felt

- **Stardew Valley is this game's ending, played as an opening.** The Joja
  cubicle - cameras over every chair, managers watching from high windows,
  walls like cells - exists only to be escaped in minute one
  ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/StardewValley)).
  Lesson: the escape lands because the CONTRAST is rendered, not scored.
  Workgrumble already renders the cubicle for dozens of hours; the ending
  only has to render the other side for one scene and the player's whole
  playtime becomes the contrast. The farm brochure on the wall is the
  grandfather's letter in reverse.
- **Papers, Please prices escape in contraband, under the employer's
  nose.** Of its 20 endings, the escape-to-Obristan family are the only
  ones that play the victory theme; they require illegally hoarding
  passports and per-family-member credits across the late days
  ([Papers Please wiki](https://papersplease.fandom.com/wiki/Endings)).
  Lessons: (a) escapes EARNED by resources accumulated inside the job's
  own systems feel transgressive and delicious - the escape fund accrued
  from the payslip is already this; (b) staying-put endings and escape
  endings sharing one presentation frame (same music cue logic) makes the
  escape read as THE win without a menu ever saying so.
- **Princess Maker / Kudos / Alter Ego: the ending is a LIFE the state
  implies.** PM2 famously resolves 8 years of stat-raising into one of ~74
  lives; Kudos runs age 20-30 and ends in what you became
  ([Kudos wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kudos_(video_game)),
  [PM2 review](https://geekysweetie.com/princess-maker-2-review-retro-pc-game-simulation-game-life-sim-anime-game/)).
  This is e11's "chosen by state, not by menu" with decades of prior art:
  the epilogue should NARRATE the career the file recorded (the tickets,
  the shops, the declined walk-outs) into the escape, not just total it.
- **Idle games prove a designed endpoint elevates a number-go-up loop.**
  Universal Paperclips and A Dark Room are beloved SPECIFICALLY for having
  a real arc and a real end in a genre built on endlessness
  ([HN thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29638011)). The
  workgrumble translation: salary-line-goes-up is the idle loop, and every
  exit ending is the player choosing to stop the loop - the rarest move in
  the genre, which is exactly why it feels like a win.
- **Felt vs score screen**: the reward literature says layered payoff -
  completion, evaluation, unlock - beats a bare total
  ([Game Developer on layered rewards](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-theory-applied-a-layered-rewards-system)).
  Concrete recipe for workgrumble: (1) a rendered morning-after vignette
  per ending (the one scene on the other side), (2) THEN the whole-career
  scorecard e11 already plans (credits roll over the career's own
  artifacts - ticket titles, review quotes, the declined offers), (3) a
  permanent trophy the OS remembers (the career card; the desktop
  wallpaper of the save slot becomes the farm). The vignette is the part
  no career sim in the reference set actually ships - it is the cheap,
  ownable differentiator.

## 3. Shortlist: recommended ending set (8)

Ordered roughly cheap -> ultimate. Two axes: CAPITAL (fund bar) and
LIFESTYLE/TIME (burn gates, endurance, prerequisites). All are offered by
state at arc boundaries per e11, never menu-picked.

1. **The van (walkabout)** - cheapest bar, finite by design; doubles as the
   NG+ seam so "the money ran out" is a story beat, not a fail state.
2. **The trade (electrician/plumber)** - low capital, priced in endurance
   (a halved-pay stretch); adds the second difficulty axis and the game's
   best punchline material about work that stays fixed.
3. **The classroom (service exit; nursing as flavor)** - small lump, gated
   on low personal burn; the warm ending, mirror-of-FIRE mechanically.
4. **Abroad (Thailand-class)** - small savings bar + an income-source
   prerequisite (side gig that survives the resignation); mid difficulty,
   mechanically distinct from every pile-of-money ending.
5. **The café (own business, physical flavor; workshop/bookstore skins)** -
   mid-size lump + a "plan" assembled from lunch-break slack windows;
   slacking with intent becomes investment.
6. **FIRE** - 25x the player's OWN rolling burn; the self-tuning ending
   where every stress-relief purchase moves the goalpost - golden
   handcuffs as arithmetic.
7. **The farm** - canonical ultimate; biggest lump, price drifts up with
   salary (lifestyle inflation curve); played 100% straight.
8. **(Contingent) The indie-SaaS** - ramen-profitable side project as an
   ending; include ONLY if the owner rules it counts as "out" (Q-A below);
   otherwise it becomes an in-game trap/flavor, not an ending.

Set property worth keeping: 1-3 are reachable by LOW earners playing
frugally, 5-7 reward high earners who resist lifestyle inflation, 4 rewards
players who built something on the side. Every player archetype has a door.

## 4. Open design questions (for the owner)

- **Q-A: does indie-SaaS count as escape?** It is the most tech-native exit
  and the easiest to write, but the player ends the game at a computer
  answering support email. Owner's frame says endings = out of IT WORK; is
  self-employed IT out? (My read: it is out of the JOB, not out of IT -
  include only if the epilogue can sell "no boss, no standup" as the actual
  escape without the tone rule breaking. The tempting "you're still at a
  terminal at 2am" gag mocks the player's dream and is OFF LIMITS under the
  tone rule.)
- **Q-B: one service ending or two?** Teaching and nursing share a knob
  shape (credential + low-burn gate). Recommend one ending with flavor
  choice; owner may want both for epilogue variety.
- **Q-C: does the FIRE number track ACTUAL in-game spend?** The 25x-of-own-
  burn knob makes stress-relief purchases literally postpone freedom -
  mechanically beautiful, but it couples the slack economy to the ending
  economy. Confirm before wiring, since it retunes every price in the slack
  shop.
- **Q-D: van ending = true ending, NG+ seam, or both?** Recommendation:
  both - taking it rolls credits; the save then offers "October" (NG+
  re-entry with records kept). This answers e11 Q2 for one ending without
  deciding it for all.
- **Q-E: currency and locale.** All real figures above are US/THB-flavored;
  the game is an English-language caricature with its own currency. Confirm
  endings are priced ONLY in game-salary units (e.g. "the farm costs ~N
  career-years of a helpdesk salary") so no real figure ever ships - which
  also discharges every DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED mark by construction.
- **Q-F: do declined endings drift?** e11 says declining is remembered. The
  farm price already drifts up with salary; should DECLINING an ending also
  raise its price (the dream recedes each time you say "one more year")?
  "One more year syndrome" is a named FIRE-community failure mode and would
  be the most true-to-life mechanic in the set.
