# Market spike: IT-career sim field (2026-07-30)

Question: is there room for a parody IT-career sim (helpdesk -> devops) in fake-OS UI, web, free?

## Field data (Steam, checked 2026-07-30)

| Game | Model | Score | Reviews | CCU now | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tech Support: Error Unknown (2019) | $, desktop | 74% | 563 | - | Chat-support sim, Papers-Please-adjacent. Praise: premise, story threads. Criticism: core ticket loop "quickly grows stale", tedious difficulty, bland presentation, interface friction |
| IT Specialist Simulator (EA 2025-11) | $13.99, desktop | 71 | 309 | 4 | DIRECT competitor: helpdesk -> IT PM career, SLAs, real commands. Serious realism tone. 2-3 yr EA roadmap. Tiny traction |
| Grey Hack (2017) | $19.99, desktop | 86 | 1,673 | 32 | Realistic hacking MMO w/ real-ish shell. Healthy niche, small |
| Bitburner (2021) | FREE, browser+Steam | 92 | 6,954 | 546 | Programming idle game. Biggest of the set BY FAR |

## Read

1. **Premise validated, execution is the gap.** TS:EU proves people buy "IT support as game" (563 reviews, mostly positive) - and its criticisms are exactly what our design answers: staleness -> parody variety + slack risk/reward loop; blandness -> caricature tone; interface friction -> the OS IS the interface, built as first-class.
2. **Direct competitor exists but hasn't taken the lane.** IT Specialist Simulator = same career-ladder concept, launched 8 months ago, 4 concurrent players. Serious-realism, paid, desktop, long EA. Lane is contested on paper, open in practice.
3. **Free + browser + depth wins this niche.** Bitburner (free, browser-playable) has 4x the reviews and 17x the CCU of Grey Hack (paid, more realistic). Strong evidence for our web/free choice.
4. **Nobody does comedy.** Entire field is earnest. Parody + slacking-under-boss-pressure mechanic = unclaimed wedge. Closest tonal comparables (Papers Please "workplace dread", Job Simulator "job parody") are both successes in adjacent spaces.

## Verdict

GO-quality wedge: **the only comedy IT-career sim, free, in browser, where slacking off is a core mechanic.** Differentiation vs the one direct competitor is tone (parody vs earnest), price (free vs $13.99), platform (browser vs Steam desktop), and loop (slack/boss risk layer they lack).
