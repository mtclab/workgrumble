# The exits - endings under the escape-from-IT frame

Owner decisions 2026-08-20/21, on the research in
`docs/research/exit-endings.md`. Supersedes the FRAME of
`docs/design/e11-endings.md` (its machinery - state-triggered offers at arc
boundaries, declining-is-remembered - survives and is built on; its four-exit
SET does not). Status: design settled enough to slice; open items at the end
are build-order questions, not owner gates.

## The frame (owner, decided)

Every ending gets the player OUT of IT work. The game's whole comedy is the
job; the ending is the day the job stops. Two rules hang off that:

- **The tone rule**: the game never mocks the ending itself. The comedy is
  the job being escaped, not the escape. (Concretely: the indie-SaaS ending
  may not do the "you are still at a terminal at 2am" gag - owner confirmed
  the ending is IN on the strength of "no boss, no standup" being played
  straight.)
- **Stalling is not legitimate play** (owner, verbatim distinction: slacking
  IS, stalling is not). An ending offered and declined has a cost - see the
  drift rule - and a player who idles at the top of the ladder with every
  door open is applying "one more year" to themselves; the game may say so.

## The set (final, 8)

Ordered cheap -> ultimate. Two axes: CAPITAL (the fund bar) and
LIFESTYLE/TIME (burn gates, endurance stretches, prerequisites). All endings
are OFFERED BY STATE at arc boundaries per e11 machinery - never menu-picked.

1. **The van (walkabout)** - cheapest bar (vehicle + runway). Finite by
   design: taking it rolls credits, and the save then offers "October" -
   NG+ re-entry with records kept. The ending that is also a beginning.
2. **The trade (electrician/plumber)** - near-zero capital, priced in
   ENDURANCE: a halved-pay apprenticeship stretch the player must sit
   through. The second difficulty axis in one ending: time and humility
   instead of money.
3. **The classroom (teaching; nursing as epilogue flavor)** - small lump
   gated on LOW PERSONAL BURN. The warm ending; mechanically the mirror of
   FIRE (you do not need the pile if you do not have the burn).
4. **Abroad (Thailand-class)** - small savings bar PLUS an income-source
   prerequisite that survives the resignation (the side thing built during
   the career). Mechanically distinct from every pile-of-money ending.
5. **The cafe (own business; workshop/bookstore epilogue skins)** - roughly
   a third of the farm price PLUS a "plan" assembled from lunch-break slack
   windows. Slacking with intent becomes investment - the slack mechanic's
   redemption arc.
6. **FIRE** - **25x the player's OWN rolling burn** (owner decided: tracks
   actual in-game spending). Every stress-relief purchase moves the
   goalpost; golden handcuffs as arithmetic; the self-tuning difficulty.
   COST: couples the slack economy to the ending economy - a slack-price
   balance pass is part of the FIRE slice, not optional.
7. **The farm** - canonical ultimate. Biggest lump; price drifts up with
   salary (lifestyle inflation as difficulty curve). Played 100% straight.
8. **The indie-SaaS** - ramen-profitable side product as the exit (owner:
   counts as out). Epilogue sells the actual escape: no boss, no standup,
   no ticket queue. Tone rule applies hardest here.

Set property (kept deliberately): 1-3 are reachable by frugal low earners,
5-7 reward high earners who resist lifestyle inflation, 4 and 8 reward
side-builders. Every playstyle has a door.

## The two standing rules

- **Decline drift (owner decided)**: declining an offered ending RAISES its
  price. "One more year syndrome" is a named FIRE-community failure mode and
  is now a mechanic: the dream recedes each time you say not yet. Composes
  with the farm's salary-linked drift (they are the same rule seen from two
  sides: the price moves because you stayed).
- **Pricing in game-salary units only**: every threshold is authored as
  career-shaped arithmetic ("~N years of a helpdesk salary", "25x your own
  burn"), never a real-world figure. This discharges every
  DO-NOT-SHIP-UNVERIFIED mark in the research by construction.

## The felt ending (from the games survey)

Three beats, all cheap, all on shipped surfaces:
1. **The morning-after vignette** - one authored scene per ending, trading
   on contrast with the cubicle the player has stared at all game (the
   Stardew/Joja lesson: the escape lands because the cage was rendered).
2. **The whole-career scorecard** - credits as the career's own numbers:
   tickets closed, bosses survived, hours padded, the fund's history.
3. **The permanent trophy** - the ending on the badge/career card, visible
   at the log-on screen forever (rides the 0.2.1 durable-account rails).

## Slicing (proposal, build-order)

- **Slice 1 - the frame + two endings**: offer machinery extended to the
  two-axis price model (capital + gate), decline drift wired, the felt-
  ending recipe built once; ship VAN (cheapest, also the NG+ seam) + FARM
  (already the north star, machinery exists). Proves the whole shape.
- **Slice 2 - the middle**: trade, classroom, abroad - the non-capital
  axes (endurance stretch, burn gate, income prerequisite).
- **Slice 3 - the coupled ones**: FIRE (with the slack-price balance
  pass), cafe (the plan-from-slack-windows meter), indie-SaaS (the side
  project surface).

## Open items (build-time, not owner gates)

- Stall pressure: where the "one more year" needling lives (weekend review
  voice vs a colleague line) - decided at slice 1 with the drift wiring.
- The service ending ships as ONE ending (classroom) with nursing as
  epilogue flavor - revisit only if epilogue variety wants it split.
- NG+ "October" scope: records kept, what exactly carries - specced with
  slice 1's van work against the 0.6.0 carry machinery.
